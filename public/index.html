Field manual

  1. Drive over letters scattered across the grassland to collect them into your tray.
  2. Spell a word with your tiles to load shells. Longer words hit much harder:
  3. Grab power-ups and deploy them when you need them.
  4. Destroy enemy tanks. Their letters spill onto the field.

⌨️ Keyboard & mouse

WASD / arrows — drive
Mouse — aim · Click / Space — fire
Enter — type a word, Enter again to load
Click tiles to spell · Right-click a tile to drop it
1–5 — power-ups · Tab — scores

📱 Touch

Left thumb — drive stick
Right thumb — aim stick (drag to fire, tap to shoot)
Tap tiles to spell · Hold a tile to drop it
Tap power-up slots to deploy
